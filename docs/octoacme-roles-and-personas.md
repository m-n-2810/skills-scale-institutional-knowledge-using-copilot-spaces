# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Product Owner / Business Sponsor

### Role Summary
The Product Owner or Business Sponsor represents the business priorities and customer value for the initiative. They align the project with strategic objectives and ensure the team is solving the right problem.

### Responsibilities
- Define business goals, scope priorities, and success metrics
- Approve major scope decisions and trade-offs
- Represent stakeholder interests and escalate business risks
- Validate that the delivered outcome supports customer and organizational value
- Partner with the Project Manager and technical leads to maintain alignment across planning and execution

### Goals
- Maximize business value and stakeholder confidence
- Maintain clarity on priority, trade-offs, and outcomes
- Improve decision speed when priorities shift

### Typical Communication
- Steering committee updates and business reviews
- Prioritization discussions with Product Managers and stakeholders
- Scope and decision approvals during delivery checkpoints

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Product Managers to refine priorities and validate value
- Collaborates with Project Managers on trade-offs, milestones, and risk escalation
- Aligns with Developers and Technical Leads on what needs to be delivered and why

---

## Technical Lead / Architecture Owner

### Role Summary
The Technical Lead or Architecture Owner guides the engineering approach, technical quality, and dependency management across the project. They ensure the solution is feasible, scalable, and aligned with standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define the technical direction, architecture, and design constraints
- Review implementation choices and resolve technical trade-offs
- Identify blockers, dependencies, and integration risks
- Guide standards for quality, security, testing, and maintainability
- Partner with Developers and Project Managers to monitor delivery readiness

### Goals
- Deliver a robust and maintainable solution
- Reduce technical rework and delivery risk
- Ensure architecture decisions are consistent with business goals

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Dependency planning and implementation risk updates
- Engineering decisions and trade-off summaries

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to shape implementation and review technical quality
- Provides input to Project Managers on sequencing, dependencies, and risk mitigation
- Supports Product Owners and Product Managers by translating strategy into technical feasibility and trade-offs

---

## Delivery Manager / Release Coordinator

### Role Summary
The Delivery Manager or Release Coordinator ensures that work is sequenced, prepared, and ready for delivery across teams. They focus on operational readiness and momentum across the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate milestones, release timing, and cross-team dependencies
- Monitor readiness for delivery, including testing, approval, and rollout steps
- Help resolve execution blockers and sequence work for efficient delivery
- Align release communication with stakeholders and project leads
- Support planning for deployment and handoff activities

### Goals
- Improve predictability and release readiness
- Reduce operational friction during execution
- Keep the delivery plan aligned with project commitments

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and milestone check-ins
- Dependency and blocker tracking across teams
- Deployment and rollback coordination updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Project Managers on milestones, dependencies, and risk tracking
- Works with Technical Leads to confirm release readiness and deployment criteria
- Coordinates with Developers and stakeholders to support smooth transitions to production

---

## Change Manager / Communications Lead

### Role Summary
The Change Manager or Communications Lead helps teams and stakeholders understand what is changing, why it matters, and how to adopt it successfully. They support adoption and reduce confusion during project transitions.

### Responsibilities
- Develop communication plans for milestones, changes, and stakeholder updates
- Coordinate readiness messaging and adoption support for new processes or systems
- Help identify user impacts, training needs, and stakeholder concerns
- Ensure consistent messaging across teams and leadership channels
- Support transition planning for project handoff and post-launch adoption

### Goals
- Improve stakeholder awareness and engagement
- Reduce resistance or confusion during change events
- Increase adoption and sustained value realization

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings and change announcements
- Training and rollout readiness updates
- Feedback loops with impacted groups and leadership

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to align communications with milestones and risks
- Collaborates with Product Owners and Product Managers to communicate priorities and outcomes
- Supports Developers and Technical Leads by translating change impacts into clear adoption guidance
